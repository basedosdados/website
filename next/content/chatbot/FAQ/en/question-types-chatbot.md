Questions about totals, rankings and trends over time, for any geographic breakdown. On its home page, the Chatbot itself gives you some examples by topic, such as:

- “How many companies were opened per month in Curitiba in 2025? Show it in a chart.”
- “Show on a map the population of each municipality in Santa Catarina in 2022.”
- “How has the number of formal jobs in my state changed over the last 10 years?”

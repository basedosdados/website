The chatbot is connected to more than 740 tables that make up the BD public datalake. It is a valuable and constantly growing source, with data on the economy (CNPJ, RAIS, Caged), health (PNS, SINAN), education (SAEB, School Census), demographics (2022 Census, PNAD), the environment and much more.
